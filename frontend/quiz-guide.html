📘 Quiz Guide

Master your quiz with these smart strategies!

1. Know Your Quiz Rules

Understand time limits, scoring, and structure before you start.

2. Focus on Accuracy

Read questions carefully and eliminate wrong answers first.

3. Don't Panic

Stay calm under pressure—breathe and take it question by question.

4. Use Time Wisely

Don’t linger too long on hard questions; mark and revisit if needed.
